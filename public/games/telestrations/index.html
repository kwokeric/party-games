How to play

  1. Everyone gets a secret, auto-generated prompt like "Astronaut Pizza."
  2. Take turns drawing what you're given, then guessing what the previous drawing shows — passed along a chain.
  3. At the end, browse back and forth through every chain to see how far each one drifted.
