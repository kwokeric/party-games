How to play

  • Everyone gets a secret, auto-generated prompt like "Astronaut Pizza."
  • Take turns drawing what you're given, then guessing what the previous drawing shows — passed along a chain.
  • At the end, watch every chain drift from the original prompt, one chain at a time.
