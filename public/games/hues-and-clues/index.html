Waiting for players to join

How to play

  • Each round, one player picks a secret color on the grid.
  • They give everyone ONE word as a clue — no color names allowed.
  • Everyone else taps the grid to guess. Closest guess scores the most: 3 pts right on, 2 pts one away, 1 pt two away, 0 otherwise.
  • Clue-giving rotates until everyone's had a turn.
