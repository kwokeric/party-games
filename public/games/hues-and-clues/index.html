How to play

  1. A clue-giver secretly picks a color on the grid.
  2. They give a one-word clue, then everyone guesses in turn.
  3. A second two-word clue gets a final guess.
  4. The final guess scores 3, 2 or 1 points for 0, 1 or 2 cells away.
  5. Clue-giver gets points for guesses within 1 cell away
