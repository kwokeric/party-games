How this piece works

  • Everyone is secretly Liberal, Fascist, or Hitler. Liberals win by shutting fascists out of power; fascists win by getting enough of their policies passed or by getting Hitler elected Chancellor once things get dicey.
  • Instead of the old "everyone close your eyes, fascists open your eyes" routine, each player's own device just shows them their role privately.
  • Fascists always see their teammates and who Hitler is. In small games (5–6 players) Hitler is told the fascist team too, since there's only one non-Hitler fascist to hide; in bigger games Hitler is kept in the dark like everyone else.
