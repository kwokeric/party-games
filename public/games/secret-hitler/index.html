How this works

  • Everyone is secretly Liberal, Fascist, or Hitler. Liberals win by shutting fascists out of power; fascists win by getting enough of their policies passed, or by getting Hitler elected Chancellor once things get dicey.
  • Check your phone for your roles. Fascists will be shown their teammates.
  • In small games (5–6 players) Hitler is told the fascist team too, since there's only one non-Hitler fascist to hide; in bigger games Hitler is kept in the dark like everyone else.
  • Needs 5–10 players. Nominating a Chancellor, voting, and the legislative session still happen out loud at the table.
