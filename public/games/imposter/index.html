How to play

  1. Everyone gets the same secret word except the imposter, who only gets a loosely related hint.
  2. Go around the circle in person. Each player says one word related to the secret word.
  3. Then everyone votes on who the imposter is. The imposter wins by staying hidden, or by guessing the word once caught.
