## 🎈 party-games

A little collection of party games to play with friends. One person opens
the lobby on their phone, shares a short room code, and everyone else joins
in from their own phones — no app to download, no accounts to make.

### The games

- **Guess the Size** — drag an object until you think it matches its real-world size next to another object. Closest guess wins.
- **Match the Color** — blend your chameleon into the background color before time runs out. Closest match wins.

More games get added over time.
